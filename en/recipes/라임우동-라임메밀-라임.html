20240822 #breakfastdiary 1155, 27°C/partly cloudy, 30min/exercise(S, M) Every summer, I eat #limeudon once or twice. The recipe gets upgraded every year, and this year it's buckwheat noodles instead of udon, with a super, super simple broth, If I had to describe it in terms of scent, it's #limebuckwheat with a charmingly bitter finish note You need buckwheat noodles, 2 limes, and tsuyu. Mix tsuyu and water in a 1:3 ratio and squeeze in 1/2 lime. Sniff sniff sniff, my hands are full of lime scent. Boil the buckwheat noodles, adding cold water about three times, then rinse thoroughly in cold water.
