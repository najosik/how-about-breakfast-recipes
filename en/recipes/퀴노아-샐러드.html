Quinoa Salad

A roughly 900kcal quinoa salad with king oyster mushroom, walnuts, and avocado on the side

Ingredients

1 cup quinoa, king oyster mushroom, 2T raisins, a little basil, 2T walnuts, (dressing) 2T white wine vinegar, 1T Dijon mustard, 3T olive oil, a pinch of salt and pepper

Steps

1. Fill a pot with plenty of water, add a pinch of salt and 1 cup quinoa, and boil. 2. In a mixing bowl, stir together 2T white wine vinegar, 1T Dijon mustard, 3T olive oil, and a pinch of salt and pepper to make the dressing. 3. Heat olive oil in a pan, add salt and pepper, and sauté the king oyster mushroom. 4. Drain the quinoa from step 1 and add to the mixing bowl from step 2, along with the mushroom from step 3, 2T raisins, and chopped basil, and mix well. Taste and add salt if needed. 5. Plate it, peel and slice 1/2 an avocado lengthwise on top, and add walnuts lightly toasted in a pan and crushed.
