20240821 #breakfastdiary 1154, 26°C/heavy rain, 25min/exercise(S, M) The #grilledbroccoli I ate so deliciously at #SunKitchen You need broccoli, (store-bought) sauerkraut, beet, and almonds. Last night before bed, I julienned some beet and tossed it into the sauerkraut. Woke up this morning to find a beautiful purple sauerkraut ready. Blanch the broccoli in boiling water. Just briefly. Drain off a bit of moisture, then stir-fry over high heat in olive oil in a hot pan. Need to cook off the moisture to make it crispy. Plate the broccoli and top with sauerkraut and almonds. Felt a bit bare, so I mixed some whole grain mustard into olive oil and drizzled it on lightly. 👆I think without boiling — either pan-frying with enough moisture cooked off, or oven-roasting — is what brings out that crispy flavor. - For various reasons, I stopped by the library over the weekend and browsed the "Anyway" series.
