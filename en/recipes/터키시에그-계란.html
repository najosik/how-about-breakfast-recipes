Steps

  1. 01

    Soft-boil the eggs.

  2. 02

    Mix the Greek yogurt with minced garlic, chopped parsley stems, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.

  3. 03

    Melt butter in a small pan, add chili paste, and simmer gently for about a minute.

  4. 04

    Place the yogurt and eggs in a bowl and top with the butter chili paste from step 3 and a little chopped parsley leaf.

  5. 05

    Eat alongside toasted sourdough.
