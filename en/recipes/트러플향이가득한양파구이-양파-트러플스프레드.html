20221106 #breakfastdiary 588, 9°C/clear, 20 min/workout I opened the truffle spread I bought a couple of days ago. It's beautifully smooth — no trace of actual truffle to be found. Checking the label: 5% black truffle oil (0.01% actual truffle). Well, of course. I minced a single shiitake mushroom, sautéed it in a pan, and mixed it with the truffle spread, and honestly, it worked out pretty convincingly. Halve the prettiest onion in the world, then sear it slowly and evenly in a hot pan with olive oil. A moderately small onion works best. Move the onion to a plate and top with the truffle spread I made. Feeling like it needs a bit more, I add chopped perilla leaves.
