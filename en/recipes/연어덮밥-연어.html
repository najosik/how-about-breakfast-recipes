Salmon Rice Bowl

2024-08-18 · 25°C/대체로 흐림, 20분/운동(M)

Notes

20240818 #breakfastdiary 1151, 25°C/mostly cloudy, 20min/exercise(M) Prepare 200g of raw salmon fillet. It varies by person, but that's just a touch short of 2 servings. You'll need a little scallion. Beyond that, prepare mirin, sesame oil, chicken stock, seasoned salt, and pepper. It's fine if you're missing one or two of these — as long as the salmon itself tastes good, that's what matters.

Ingredients

200g salmon, 1T scallion, 2T mirin, 2T sesame oil, 1/2t chicken stock, 1/2t seasoned salt, pepper

Steps

1. Cut the salmon into long strips, put it in a bowl with the scallion (white part), 2T mirin, 2T sesame oil, 1/2t chicken stock, 1/2t seasoned salt, and a little pepper, and mix well. 2. Since I won't be using sushi vinegar today, season the salmon to taste with the (unbeatable) seasoned salt.
