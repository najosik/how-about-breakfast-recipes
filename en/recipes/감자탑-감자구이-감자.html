20241020 #breakfastdiary 1211, 17°C/clear, 25min/exercise(S, M) If you translate pyramid into Chinese, it becomes 金字塔 (geumjatap, 'gold-character tower'). Apparently the character 金 (gold) looks similar to a pyramid shape, so it became a tower shaped like the character 金, hence 金字塔. Today's breakfast is slices of potato tossed in a butter and garlic sauce, stacked layer by layer like a #potatotower, then roasted in the oven to make #roastedpotato with the layers still visible. In modern usage, geumjatap is used to mean 'a great achievement to be remembered by later generations' rather than its original meaning, and honestly that phrase fits this dish's flavor really well.
