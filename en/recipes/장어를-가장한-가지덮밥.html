#Eggplant Rice Bowl Playing at Eel

2025-08-31 · 26°C/대체로 맑음, 20분/운동(M) · 445kcal

Ingredients

brown rice, 2 eggplants, oil, a little sesame oil and sesame seeds, (sauce) 2T dark soy sauce, 2T mirin, 1T sugar, 1t minced ginger, 4T water

Steps

1. Wash 2 eggplants thoroughly, wrap in plastic wrap, and microwave for 2 minutes. (Don't peel — the skin is full of fiber.) 2. Mix 2T dark soy sauce, 2T mirin, 1T sugar, 1t minced ginger, and 4T water together to make the sauce. 3. The eggplant fresh out of the microwave is very hot. Cut it in half, spread it open, score it so it soaks up the sauce well, then cook slowly in an oiled pan, brushing on the sauce from step 3 as it cooks. 5. Place the eggplant from step 3 over brown rice and keep it simple with a little sesame oil and sesame seeds on top.
