Eggplant Rice Bowl Masquerading as Eel

Ingredients

Check items off while grocery shopping - it’s remembered here.

Steps

  1. 01

    Wash the 2 eggplants thoroughly, cover with plastic wrap, and microwave for 2 minutes. (Don't peel the fiber-rich skin.)

  2. 02

    Combine 2T soy sauce, 2T mirin, 1T sugar, 1t minced ginger, and 4T water, and mix well to make the sauce.

  3. 03

    The eggplant fresh out of the microwave is very hot. Cut it in half, spread it open, score it so the sauce soaks in well, then slowly grill it in an oiled pan while brushing on the sauce from step 2.

  4. 04

    Place the eggplant from step 3 over the brown rice, and finish simply with a little sesame oil and sesame seeds.
