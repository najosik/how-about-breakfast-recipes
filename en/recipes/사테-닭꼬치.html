Singapore-style satay sauce is peanut-based with a tangy-sweet character. Not sure why I even had this at home, but there was a store-bought sauce, so I used it. Marinated from midnight last night for about 6 hours — looks like Singapore-style #satay, but in the end it's really just #chickenskewers

Ingredients

Chicken on skewers, (Singapore-style) satay sauce, scallion, cherry tomatoes, oil, pepper

Steps

1. Line a tray with parchment paper, place the chicken skewers, brush with satay sauce, and marinate overnight. 2. Grill the chicken skewers on a pan, brushing with vegetable oil as you go. 3. Skewer the scallion and tomato as well, sprinkle with pepper, and grill.
