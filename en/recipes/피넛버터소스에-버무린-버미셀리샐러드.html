#Vermicelli Salad Tossed in Peanut Butter Sauce

Ingredients

red cabbage, cucumber, tomato, carrot, a moderate amount of bok choy, vermicelli for 2 servings, (dressing) 3T peanut butter, 1T soy sauce, 2T lime juice, 2T honey, 1T sesame oil, 1t minced garlic, 3T water

Steps

1. Julienne the red cabbage, cucumber, tomato, and carrot thin. 2. Mix 3T peanut butter, 1T soy sauce, 2T lime juice, 2T honey, 1T sesame oil, 1t minced garlic, and 3T water together to make the dressing. 3. Soak the vermicelli in water for about 30 minutes, blanch in boiling water for 2-3 minutes, then rinse quickly in cold water and drain. 4. Arrange the red cabbage, cucumber, tomato, carrot, and bok choy in a bowl, pile the vermicelli in the middle, pour over the dressing from step 2, and toss well. Adding chopped pistachios or peanuts would add a fun bit of texture too.
