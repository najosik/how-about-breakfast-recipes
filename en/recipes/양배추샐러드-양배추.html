White Wine-Braised Cabbage Salad

#레시피#미라클모닝#양배추#양배추샐러드#조식#조식다이어리

Notes

20230612 #breakfastdiary 781, 19°C/mostly cloudy, 21min/workout(Y) Drawn in by the story of a grandmother in her 70s who started a cooking vlog as rehab for an illness (something like dementia), I found my way to @hakuraidou. (More thoughts on that another time.) Simmered in white wine, cabbage takes on just the right tang. Short on milk, I made the cream sauce with soy milk instead, and it turned out to have its own distinct flavor. 1. Cut the cabbage into wedges and sear the cut sides in a hot pan with olive oil. 2. Pour in white wine, cover, and let it simmer down over low heat. 3. Make a (soy-milk) cream sauce, pour it over the cabbage, and finish with plenty of pepper. #CabbageSalad Movies + Breakfast I've strayed from my original spirit and started focusing only on breakfast, though that was never really what this account was about.
