Shrimp and Kabocha Squash Salad

(Over low heat — it needs longer than you'd think.) In the same pan, add the shrimp and cook with salt, salt, pepper, pepper, herbs, herbs. (Searing the shrimp first — wouldn't that let its flavor seep into the squash?) Plate the squash and shrimp, then crush in some walnuts with a satisfying crunch.
