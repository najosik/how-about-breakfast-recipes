Nasi Dagang Avocado Someone Else Made My Breakfast

2025-05-10 · 29°C/대체로 흐림, 20분/운동(M)

Notes

20250510 #breakfastdiary 1396, 29°C/mostly cloudy, 20 min/exercise (M) #NasiDagang is a traditional dish and iconic breakfast from eastern Malaysia. The rice is steamed with coconut milk, fenugreek seeds, ginger, shallots, and more. It's typically soaked in water overnight, then steamed twice with coconut milk and spices, giving it a nutty flavor with a slightly sticky texture. The fish curry is usually made with tuna or mackerel, flavored with coconut milk, lemongrass, galangal, chili, turmeric, and various other spices. It's served alongside a refreshing pickle of vegetables like cucumber and carrot, cured in vinegar and sugar. A spicy shrimp paste sauce is served on the side too, for an extra hit of umami! In some regions or restaurants, boiled eggs and toasted coconut are also served. -

Ingredients

1 avocado, 1/2 onion, salt, pepper, red wine vinegar (glug glug glug glug glug), olive oil (glug glug)

Steps

1. The avocado is perfectly ripe. Peel and slice into long pieces. 2. Peel the onion and slice into rounds. 3. Arrange the avocado and onion on a plate and sprinkle with a little salt and pepper. 4. Pour on red wine vinegar — glug glug glug glug glug — and olive oil — glug glug. Regional differences • The Kelantan style uses a reddish long-grain rice, while the Terengganu style mixes white glutinous rice with fragrant rice. Terengganu style rice is whiter and glossier. • The spice blend and accompaniments for the fish curry also vary slightly by region. Cultural meaning: Nasi Dagang is a dish that draws on the rich rice, seafood, and spices of Malaysia's east coast, symbolizing the country's diverse and abundant food culture. It's simple yet highly nutritious, and fits equally well for special occasions or everyday meals. Summary: Nasi Dagang is an iconic traditional dish from Malaysia's east coast, made up of rice steamed with coconut milk and spices, tuna or mackerel curry, pickled vegetables, and shrimp paste. It's a well-rounded one-bowl meal that harmoniously combines nuttiness, spiciness, and tang.
