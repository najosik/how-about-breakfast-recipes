King Oyster Mushroom Salad

2025-12-29 · 3°C/대체로 흐림 · 420kcal

Notes

20251229 #breakfastdiary day 1598, 3°C/mostly cloudy 420kcal King Oyster Mushroom Salad, just like chicken breast

Ingredients

3 king oyster mushrooms, 1T olive oil, 1T mayonnaise, 1t whole grain mustard, 1t tsuyu, a little chopped chives, black sesame

Steps

1. Brush the dust off the king oyster mushrooms and shred them with a fork — the texture is just like chicken breast. 2. Heat a pan, add olive oil, and quickly sauté the chicken-breast-like king oyster mushroom over high heat. 3. In a bowl, combine the chicken-breast-like king oyster mushroom with 1T mayonnaise, 1t whole grain mustard, 1t tsuyu, and chopped chives, and mix well. 2. Top with sliced tomato in a bowl and sprinkle with a little black sesame.
