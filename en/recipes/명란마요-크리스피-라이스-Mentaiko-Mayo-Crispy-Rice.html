#1804

Mentaiko Mayo Crispy Rice

2026-07-28 · 25°C/cloudy · 450kcal

Notes

20260728, #BreakfastDiary 1793, 25°C/cloudy About 450kcal, Mentaiko Mayo Crispy Rice.

Ingredients

1/2 bowl rice, 5g butter and 1T cooking oil, mentaiko and scallion, a little grana padano, (mentaiko mayo) 2T mayonnaise, 1T mentaiko, oligo syrup and mirin, a little pepper

Steps

1. Take the rice out ahead of time to cool slightly, wrap in plastic wrap, press flat to about 1cm thick, and cut into bite-size pieces. 2. Heat oil and butter in a pan and fry the rice from step 1 on both sides until crisp, like frying. 3. Mix 2T mayonnaise, 1T mentaiko, oligo syrup, mirin, and a little pepper together to make the mentaiko mayo. 4. Top the rice from step 1 with 1/2T of the mentaiko mayo from step 3, chopped scallion, more mentaiko, and a little grana padano on top.
