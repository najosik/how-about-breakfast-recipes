Mushroom Salad with Soy Sauce Dressing

Ingredients

1/4 head broccoli, 1 egg, 2 cups mushrooms, 1 clove garlic, sauce (1T soy sauce, 2T balsamic), 3-4 lettuce leaves, 1T olive oil, a pinch of pepper

Steps

1. Blanch the broccoli in boiling water. 2. Soft-boil the egg. 3. Brush the dust off the mushrooms and cut into bite-sized pieces. 4. Crush and mince 1 clove of garlic with a knife. 5. Mix 1 part soy sauce and 2 parts balsamic to make the sauce. 6. Heat olive oil in a pan, sauté the mushrooms from step 3, then add the minced garlic from step 4 and the sauce from step 5 and sauté, then add the broccoli and sauté a little longer. 7. Plate the lettuce cut into bite-sized pieces, the mixture sautéed in step 6, and the boiled egg, then add olive oil and pepper.
