Blueberry Overnight Oatmeal

Ingredients

Check items off while grocery shopping - it’s remembered here.

Steps

  1. 01

    In a blender, combine 1 cup blueberries, 2T Greek yogurt, 1T honey, 1t vanilla extract, 1T lemon juice, and 1t salt, and blend.

  2. 02

    Put 2 cups oatmeal and 2T chia seeds in a bowl, pour in the mixture from step 1, and let it sit in the fridge overnight.

  3. 03

    Top with blueberries, chopped pistachios, and peanut butter. Add a little Maldon salt.
