20210118 Anna was a scientist in the early days of humanity's space colonization era, researching deep-freezing technology that put the human body into cryosleep. When a cost-cutting government policy cut off any way to reach her family on a planet tens of thousands of light-years away, she used deep-freezing to extend her own life by over a hundred years, never letting go of her dream of one day meeting her family again — even though they would surely already be dead by then. A future where deep-freezing has become routine. Say you're diagnosed with an incurable illness, and you're told that if you go into deep freeze for 20 or 30 years, a cure will likely be waiting when you wake. Would you say goodbye to your family, friends, and colleagues and go to sleep? Or would you choose to accept the incurable illness as it is, right now? The colorful vegetable chips on today's plate, which went through their own deep-freezing (freezing), suddenly look a little different to me.
