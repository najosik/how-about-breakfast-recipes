A Weird Little Breakfast, Office Worker Style

2021-01-06

Notes

20210106 'Wae-an-dwaen-de' (a deliberately misspelled way of writing 'why not') A while back, this was the top comment on a tag called 'let's try to annoy someone in four syllables.' It's four syllables guaranteed to enrage anyone who can't rest easy typing a KakaoTalk message without immediately correcting a typo on the very next line. Honestly, even with these short breakfast posts, I'm always a little worried — is there an awkward sentence in here somewhere, a spacing mistake, or will this simple breakfast just get laughed at as a nonsensical #weirddish? No time to make gnocchi from scratch, so I buy it ready-made. It looks nice and it's cheap. Best of all, the recipe doesn't call for heavy cream, which makes it a great fit for breakfast. -Blanch the gnocchi in boiling water with a little olive oil. -Drain well in a sieve, then pan-fry with olive oil, salt, and pepper. -Add the prettiest boiled green peas in the world, a little butter, parsley flakes, and a squeeze of lemon juice. Finish with parmesan~
