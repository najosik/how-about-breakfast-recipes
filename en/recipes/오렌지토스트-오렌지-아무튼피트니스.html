Ingredients

1 sourdough, 2 oranges, 1T cream cheese, a little pistachio and mint, olive oil, a pinch of Maldon salt

Steps

1. Brush olive oil on the sourdough and toast both sides until crispy. 2. Spread a generous amount of cream cheese on the sourdough and sprinkle on today's kick — Maldon salt. On top, layer the two kinds of thinly sliced orange, chopped pistachio, honey, and mint in that order.
