So it's #YamWoonsen again. -Slice celery, onion, and tomato into a bowl. Finely chop the world's prettiest #cilantro and some scallion. (No chives on hand.) -Blanch the glass noodles (made from konjac) in boiling water and drain, then — no need to boil fresh water — blanch the squid, shrimp, and ground pork in the same pot. -Toss everything with a dressing of fish sauce, palm sugar, lime juice, a little minced garlic, and 2 crushed peperoncino peppers. -Top with a little peanuts and cilantro.
