Avocado Sandwich, Morning Ritual, Morning Routine

2021-08-31 · 22°C/소나기

Notes

20210831 #breakfastdiary 196, 22°C/showers Toast thick slices of bread on both sides in a pan with olive oil. Since I’m already at it, go heavy on the mayonnaise, pile on scrambled eggs and sliced avocado, and finish with lemon juice, lemon zest, and a little salt and pepper. #avocadosandwich On Miller’s planet, where the Endurance lands in its search for humanity’s new home, the crew gets swept up in giant tidal waves and loses a staggering 23 years, 4 months, and 8 days of Earth time. Just as one hour on Miller’s planet equals seven years on Earth, one minute on the morning commute can feel like a full hour on any ordinary day. And that feeling is even stronger on a morning when you think you woke up at the usual time, only to be unsettled by the strangely different air outside — and then realize, with a jolt, that you actually woke up exactly one hour late.
