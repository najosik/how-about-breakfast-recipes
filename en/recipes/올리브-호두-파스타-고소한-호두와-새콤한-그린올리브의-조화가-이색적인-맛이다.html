Olive Walnut Pasta. The pairing of nutty walnuts and tangy green olives makes for an unusual, distinctive flavor.

2025-06-08 · 21°C/흐림, 20분/운동(M) · 600kcal

The pairing of nutty walnuts and tangy green olives makes for an unusual, distinctive flavor.

Ingredients

2.5 cups penne (any short pasta works), 1 cup walnuts, 1 cup green olives, 1 lemon, olive oil, salt, pepper, 1 clove garlic

Steps

1. Boil the penne in salted water. 2. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the walnuts and green olives for 3-4 minutes. Once the pan is filled with a fragrant aroma, turn off the heat and add minced garlic, salt, and pepper, mixing evenly. 3. Add the penne and 1/2 cup pasta water, and mix evenly. Adjust the seasoning with salt and pasta water. 4. Turn off the heat and add lemon zest and the juice of 1/2 a lemon. 5. Plate it up and top with a little grana padano cheese.
