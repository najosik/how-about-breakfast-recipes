Asparagus Gribiche Toast

2024-08-23 · 26°C/대체로 맑음, 20분/운동(M)

Notes

20240823 #breakfastdiary 1156, 26°C/mostly clear, 20min/exercise (M) Sourdough kept in the freezer and thawed a little at a time is a great breakfast staple. Fresh out of the freezer, brushing it with olive oil and toasting it for about 5 minutes works fine, but if you take it out before bed, it's in perfect shape to pan-toast by morning. Today's breakfast, asparagus gribiche toast, needs sourdough, 4-5 stalks of asparagus, 2 eggs, a little capers, and mayonnaise. Brush both sides of the sourdough with olive oil and toast in a pan until crisp. Hard-boil the eggs, taking your time. Trim the asparagus's tough stems with a peeler; cut the stems into 1cm pieces and the tips (about 10cm) separately, then sauté in a hot pan with olive oil. In a bowl, combine the egg, asparagus stems, a good pinch of salt, a good pinch of pepper, mayonnaise, and today's kick — capers — and mix well. (Set the tips aside separately.) Let's call this asparagus gribiche. Top the sourdough with the gribiche, the asparagus tips, and a final pinch of pepper. Ingredients

- 2 slices sourdough - (asparagus gribiche) 2 eggs, 3 stalks asparagus, 2T mayonnaise, 2t capers, salt, pepper

Steps

1. Brush the sourdough with olive oil and toast both sides. 2. Hard-boil 2 eggs in boiling water for about 10 minutes. 3. Trim the asparagus stems and cut into 1cm pieces. Cut the tips (to use as topping) into 10cm pieces, then sauté in a pan with olive oil, salt, and pepper. 4. In a mixing bowl, combine the egg from step 2, the asparagus stems from step 3, 2T mayonnaise, 2t capers, salt, and pepper, and mix well. 5. Top the sourdough from step 1 with the asparagus gribiche from step 4 and the reserved asparagus tips from step 3. Finish with pepper.
