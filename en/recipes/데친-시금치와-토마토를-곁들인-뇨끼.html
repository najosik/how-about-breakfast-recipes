Gnocchi with Blanched Spinach and Tomato

2026-01-18 · -3°C/cloudy · 750kcal

Notes

Since this is the first time I've made this breakfast. About 750kcal, Gnocchi with Blanched Spinach and Tomato.

Ingredients

1 cup gnocchi, 2 bunches spinach, 3 tomatoes, 1/4 onion, 2 cloves garlic, olive oil, a pinch of salt, pepper, 1t butter, a little grana padano cheese

Steps

1. Cook the gnocchi in boiling water. Once the gnocchi float to the top, scoop them out into a pan and pan-fry until crisp, sprinkling with parmesan cheese. Set aside in a bowl. 2. Heat olive oil in a pan, sauté the chopped onion and garlic, then add the cherry tomatoes and sauté a bit more. 3. Add the crispy gnocchi and prepped spinach to step 2, season with salt, add a little pepper, and coat lightly with butter. 4. Plate step 3 and top with shaved grana padano cheese.
