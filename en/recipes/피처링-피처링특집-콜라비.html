Featuring Special: Kohlrabi Som Tam & Avocado Crostini

2020-11-22

Notes

20201122, #Featuring originally means a singer other than the main artist singing a specific part of a song, or playing an instrument on it — and it's also sometimes used deliberately, bringing in a hugely famous artist to feature on a track just to pull in that artist's fans. With the belief that swapping in an unexpected ingredient, when you don't have the one a dish is normally known for, can produce surprisingly unique results, today's breakfast is a #featuringspecial Som Tam (feat. Kohlrabi) 1. Julienne the prettiest #kohlrabi in the world along with carrot, and prepare blanched #greenbeans. 2. Combine palm sugar (1), fish sauce (1), lime juice (1) with 2-3 bird's eye chilies and tomato, and mash together. 3. Toss the vegetables from step 1 with the mixture from step 2 and fried shallots. (Kohlrabi in place of green papaya, bird's eye chilies in place of Thai chili, fried shallots in place of dried shrimp) Avocado Crostini (feat. Sweet Potato & Som Tam Dressing) 1. Heat olive oil in a pan and lightly pan-fry sweet potato slices on both sides. 2. Top the sweet potato with tomato that's been marinated in the kohlrabi som tam dressing, 3. Slice avocado on top, then finish with a light drizzle of som tam dressing (Sweet potato in place of bread, som tam dressing in place of the usual olive oil/white wine vinegar dressing)
