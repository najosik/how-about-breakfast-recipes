Microwave Egg Bread and Tomato Soup

Though if COVID keeps going like this, maybe someday this kind of hack cooking will get treated with the same reverence as the #EumsikDimibang. *Eumsik Dimibang: a roughly 400-year-old Korean-script cookbook left behind by Lady Jang of Andong, containing 146 recipes — the first of its kind. Egg bread 1. Mix pancake mix and water at a 2:1 ratio, with a little salt. 2. Put the mixture in a mug, then crack an egg on top. 3. Microwave for 3 minutes. 4. Around 2 minutes 30 seconds, you'll hear a pop. The yolk bursts and triggers a chain reaction, exploding the egg bread. Plate whatever survives the explosion. Tomato soup 1. Halve the cherry tomatoes. 2. 5 spoons of water 3. A little butter, salt, pepper. (Olive oil can substitute for the butter, but butter gives a slightly more refined flavor) 5. Microwave for 3 minutes.

Steps

  1. 01

    (Egg bread)

  2. 02

    In a large mug, combine the pancake mix and water at a 2:1 ratio with a pinch of salt.

  3. 03

    Crack an egg on top of the batter.

  4. 04

    Microwave for 3 minutes. (If the yolk isn't broken) around 2 minutes 30 seconds, the yolk may burst with a pop.

  5. 05

    Plate whatever survives the explosion.

  6. 06

    (Tomato soup)

  7. 07

    Trim the stems off the cherry tomatoes and halve them.

  8. 08

    Place cut-side up in a heatproof container and pour in 5 tbsp water.

  9. 09

    Add the butter and sprinkle with salt and pepper. Olive oil can substitute for the butter, but butter gives a slightly more refined flavor.

  10. 10

    Cover with a plate or lid (leaving one side slightly open to vent steam) and microwave for 3 minutes.

  11. 11

    Let cool slightly, then mash the tomatoes with a fork and mix into the broth.
