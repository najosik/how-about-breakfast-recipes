Root Vegetable Quinoa Salad

2020-11-26

Notes

20201126, Hi, Elon! You've seen 'The Martian,' right? The one where Watney, stranded alone on Mars, manages to grow potatoes and eventually makes his way back to Earth after all sorts of trouble. I heard you're planning to actually send humans to Mars by 2025, so just in case, SpaceX is definitely going to need some famine-relief crops. I'd actually recommend sweet potatoes over regular potatoes. In terms of how many people a given area can feed, corn supports 1 person, wheat 1.6, rice 2.4 — but sweet potatoes support 3.9. On top of that, sweet potatoes grow well even in poor soil and are rich in antioxidants, fiber, and potassium, so they'd help with skin aging from Mars's harsh air, and also with the stress-eating that comes from an irregular schedule and homesickness for Earth. Take this recipe with you. I'm rushing out the door for work, so I'll keep this short. November 26, 2020 Na-Josik -Combine #quinoa and water at a 1:2 ratio, add a little olive oil and salt, and boil. -In a heated pan, toast sliced almonds, then pan-fry the sweet potato, then add olive oil and pan-fry the lotus root. -Plate the quinoa, sweet potato, and lotus root, then dress with olive oil, white wine vinegar, and whole-grain mustard. -Top with red onion, almonds, cilantro, parsley, and lots of pepper.
