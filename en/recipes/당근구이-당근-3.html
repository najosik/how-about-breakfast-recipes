Roasted Carrots

2024-01-12 · -3°C/밁음, 21분/운동(Y)

Notes

20240112 #breakfastdiary 950, -3°C/clear, 21min/workout(Y) Well, I find myself believing less and less that anything in this world can be stated with certainty — but (even so) this really is the best recipe for taking in the fat-soluble beta-carotene carrots are so rich in. #roastedcarrots with Greek yogurt Heat olive oil in a pan and slice and roast a whole carrot, slowly, over low heat. Spoon Greek yogurt onto a plate, add the just-roasted carrot and chopped parsley, then — since it felt a little bare — pink peppercorns and a crack of black pepper. A vinaigrette would be nice on top too, but the flavor's already so full I left it off (honestly, I just forgot).
