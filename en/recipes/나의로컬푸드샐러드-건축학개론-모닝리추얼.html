Steps

  1. 01

    Wash the zucchini and cut lengthwise in half. Scoop out the seeds with a spoon to create a hollow.

  2. 02

    Heat a pan with 1 tsp olive oil and place the zucchini cut-side down, grilling over low heat for 3-4 minutes. Flip, cover, and cook 2-3 minutes more. Don't let the inside get too soft.

  3. 03

    Finely chop the tomato. Mix the olive oil, white wine vinegar, salt, and pepper to make the vinaigrette.

  4. 04

    In a bowl, combine the boiled lentils, quinoa, and tomato, and toss with the vinaigrette.

  5. 05

    Fill the grilled zucchini with the mixture from step 4 and finish with a grind of pepper.
