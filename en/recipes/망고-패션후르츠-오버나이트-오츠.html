Mango #PassionFruit Overnight Oats

2025-09-10 · 20°C/맑음, 20분/운동(M) · 376kcal

Notes

20250910 #breakfastdiary day 1504, 20°C/clear, 20min/exercise (M) Managing my diet ahead of an upcoming health checkup. This week's breakfasts will mostly be liquid/soft foods. Making these, I've found that overnight oats — a 'breakfast made by the night before' — have the advantage of making the morning commute feel more spacious. ☝️Curious about 'breakfast made by the night'? Follow the ongoing Brunch Book series, Everyday Breakfast, via the profile link. Passion fruit often gets an odd reaction because it looks like frog eggs, but it's actually a really charming fruit. There's a lot to say especially about its name — I'll save that for another time. Today's passion fruit is domestic, from Jeju Island. Things have really come to that point now. Because of its tart-sweet flavor, it pairs well with dairy like milk and yogurt — so I loaded it with milk and Greek yogurt and let it sit overnight: an approx. 376kcal mango #passionfruit overnight oats

Ingredients

1 cup oatmeal, 2t chia seeds, 1 cup milk, 1 cup Greek yogurt, 6T finely chopped mango, 1T maple syrup, 1t vanilla extract, a pinch of salt, 2 passion fruit

Steps

1. In a bowl, combine 1 cup oatmeal, 2t chia seeds, 1 cup milk, 1 cup Greek yogurt, 6T chopped mango, 1T maple syrup, 1t vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt, and mix thoroughly. Refrigerate overnight. 2. Put the flesh of 1 passion fruit into a bowl, pour the oatmeal from step 1 over it, and top with the flesh of the second passion fruit.
